What EchoAI does

  • Standard TTE and TOE study sequences, view by view, with probe position and adequacy criteria.
  • Normal values and severity thresholds for chamber size, systolic and diastolic function, and valve disease.
  • Structures → measurements → how to obtain them, so you can go from anatomy to technique.
  • Calculators: AVA by continuity, RVSP, stroke volume and cardiac output, LV mass, E/e′, PISA/EROA, BSA.
  • Interactive algorithms for diastolic function, aortic stenosis severity, and pulmonary hypertension probability.

Choosing your guideline society

The society selector is the heart of the app. Pick ASE, BSE or EACVI in Settings, or from the badge in the top-right of any content screen, and every value, threshold and algorithm branch follows that society. Where the societies genuinely disagree, the value carries a small society tag so the difference is visible rather than hidden.

Spelling

Settings → Spelling switches between British and American medical spelling (oesophageal/esophageal, TOE/TEE). "Match device" follows your device region.

Favourites and sync

Tap the star on any screen to collect it on the Home tab. Favourites and settings sync through your own iCloud account when you are signed in, so they follow you between iPhone, iPad and Mac. Nothing is stored on a server I control.

Appearance

The app is dark by default — it is meant to be used in a dim echo lab. The machine tint (wheat, ice blue, copper, phosphor green, magenta, monochrome) mirrors the colour maps on echo machines and recolours the whole app.

Common questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. All content is bundled in the app; nothing needs a network connection.

Is this a medical device?

No. EchoAI is a reference tool for trained healthcare professionals. It summarises published guidelines and performs standard calculations. It does not provide diagnoses and is not a substitute for clinical judgement or the original guideline documents. Always verify critical values against the source publications and your local protocols.

Where do the values come from?

Each screen cites the guideline it follows for the selected society. Every value is checked against that source by a clinician before it ships.

What about the AI features?

Image and loop analysis using your own AI provider key is in development. When it arrives, it will be opt-in, use a key you supply, and be clearly separated from the guideline reference content.

I found a value I disagree with.

Please tell me. Guidelines are revised, and different institutions apply them differently — corrections with a citation are always welcome.
