EchoAI Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026

The short version: EchoAI has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I collect nothing. Nothing you type into a calculator leaves your device, and no patient data should be entered into the app in the first place.

What stays on your device

The entire reference — guideline values, views, formulae, calculators and algorithms — is bundled inside the app. It works with no network connection. Your chosen guideline society, spelling, appearance and machine tint are stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your favourites and settings sync through your own private iCloud storage so they appear on your other Apple devices. That data lives in your Apple account: I cannot see it, access it or retrieve it. Signing out of iCloud, or turning off iCloud for EchoAI in your device settings, keeps everything local.

Calculator inputs and patient data

Values you type into a calculator are used to produce a result on screen and are not stored, transmitted or logged. EchoAI is not designed to hold patient records: please do not enter names, identifiers or any other patient-identifiable information.

Future AI analysis

Image and loop analysis is in development and is not part of the app today. When it ships it will be opt-in and will use an AI provider key that you supply. In that case, the images you choose to analyse would be sent directly from your device to that provider under their privacy policy and terms — not through any server of mine. This page will be updated before that feature is released.

What EchoAI does not do

  • No accounts, profiles or sign-ins
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No collection, sale or sharing of personal data with anyone

Purchases

EchoAI is a paid app bought through the App Store. Apple handles the transaction; I receive sales figures from Apple, never payment details or your identity.

Clinical use

EchoAI is a reference tool for trained healthcare professionals. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnoses, and is not a substitute for clinical judgement or the original guideline documents. Values shown depend on the guideline society selected in the app and should be verified against the cited source and your local protocols.

Children

EchoAI is intended for healthcare professionals and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
